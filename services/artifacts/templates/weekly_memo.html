Methodology
VaR · Value at Risk
Historical simulation over ninety trading days. Uses close-to-close log-returns; no parametric shape is assumed. Jorion (2007).
Expected Shortfall
Average of losses beyond the 95th VaR percentile. Coherent under sub-additivity. Acerbi & Tasche (2002).
Covariance · Ledoit-Wolf
Ledoit-Wolf linear shrinkage estimator is used where a full covariance is required — stabilises eigenvalues on thin samples.
Volatility · GKYZ
Garman-Klass-Yang-Zhang range-based estimator. Efficient under five-minute sampling, robust to opening jumps.
Max Drawdown
Minimum running difference between mark and running high, over the twelve-week window on daily marks.
